Text

A：你好！
B：𠢕早，食飽未？
A：有喔，多謝。
B：你叫啥物名？
A：我是阿明。你咧？你叫啥？
B：我叫美玲。
A：歹勢，我欲先走矣。
B：無要緊，再會！
A：再會！

A: Lí hó!
B: Gâu-tsá, tsia̍h-pá--buē?
A: Ū, to-siā.
B: Lí kiò siánn-mih miâ?
A: Guá sī A-bîng. Lí leh? Lí kiò siánn?
B: Guá kiò Bí-lîng.
A: Pháinn-sè, guá beh sing tsáu ah.
B: Bô iàu-kín, tsài-huē!
A: Tsài-huē!

Translation

A: Hello!
B: Good morning, have you eaten (how are you)?
A: I'm good, thank you.
B: What is your name.
A: I'm A-bing. How about you? What is your name?
B: I'm called Bi-ling
A: Excuse, I have to leave now.
B: No worries, see you!
A: See you!
